# Mahonemotes – Emote Wheel

A radial emote menu inspired by console-style wheels. Hold a key, flick the mouse in a direction, release – done.

## How it works
- Bind a key under **Key Bindings → Mahonemotes** (e.g. `Z`).
- **Hold** the key: the wheel opens at your cursor.
- **Move** the mouse in a direction: 12 slots of 30° each. Up = *Hello*, down = *Bye*, right = *Yes*, left = *No*.
- **Release** to perform the emote. Release in the center to cancel.
- **Tap** the key instead: the wheel stays open, left-click (or press the key again) to choose, right-click or Escape to close.

## Spiral pages
Keep rotating **clockwise past 12 o'clock** and the next page appears; rotate back to return. The mouse wheel also flips pages.

- Page 1 – everyday: hello, thanks, cheer, yes, laugh, wave, bye, sorry, cry, no, sigh, bow
- Page 2 – fun: dance, flex, roar, applaud, kiss, flirt, chicken, joke, rofl, shrug, silly, salute
- Page 3 – group: charge, follow, incoming, open fire, heal me, oom, wait, help, flee, train, attack my target, good luck

## Commands
- `/memote` – help
- `/memote size <80-300>` – wheel radius
- `/memote check` – verify emote tokens against your client
- `/memote reset` – reset settings

Works in combat (emotes are not protected actions). No dependencies, no libraries.

*Labels are currently German; English localization is planned.*
