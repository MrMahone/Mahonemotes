# Changelog

## 0.2.0

## 0.1.0
- First release: radial emote wheel for WoW Forever
- 12 slots per page (30° each), 3 pages: everyday, fun, group
- Spiral paging: rotate clockwise past 12 o'clock for the next page; mouse wheel also pages
- Hold key + release to emote, or tap to keep the wheel open and click
- `/memote size`, `/memote check`, `/memote reset`
